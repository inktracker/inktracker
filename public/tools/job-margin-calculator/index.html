That's $5.46 profit per piece, on a cost of $8.54 per piece all in. To hit a 40% margin at that cost, you'd need to charge $14.24 per piece instead of $14.00.

Margin is profit divided by what the customer pays; markup is profit divided by what the job cost you — the same number reads very differently either way, which is why "50% margin" and "50% markup" aren't the same price. One-time costs (setup, screens, digitizing) get spread across the whole run, so they matter a lot more on a short run than a long one.
