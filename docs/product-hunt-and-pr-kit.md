# InkTracker — Product Hunt Launch + Founder-Story PR Kit

Two backlink-and-traffic sources that also build brand. Copy is drafted in your voice — tweak and go.

## Part 1 — Product Hunt launch

A PH launch gets you a spike of traffic, a dofollow backlink, social proof, and often signups. Best on a Tue/Wed/Thu, 12:01am PT.

**Name:** InkTracker
**Tagline (60 char max):** Shop software for screen printers — built by a printer
*(alt: "Run your print shop: quotes, production, invoicing, QuickBooks")*

**Description (first comment / "maker's comment"):**
> Hey Product Hunt 👋 I'm Joe — I run a screen printing shop, and I got tired of running it out of spreadsheets and re-typing everything into QuickBooks. The software I tried never fit how a print shop actually works, so I built the thing I wanted.
>
> InkTracker does the whole job in one place: quoting with **live blank pricing pulled straight from S&S and AS Colour**, a production board the whole shop can see, invoicing with a real **two-way QuickBooks sync**, and an embeddable quote wizard so customers can start orders on your own site. One flat price, everything included, unlimited employees.
>
> It's built by someone who's actually pulling prints, and we run our own shop on it every day. Would genuinely love feedback from other makers and printers — what's missing, what you'd want. Happy to answer anything in the comments.
>
> 14-day free trial, no card: inktracker.app

**Topics/tags:** SaaS, Small Business, Productivity, Design Tools
**Gallery:** use your real UI screenshots (production board, quoting, invoices, wizard) + the hero shop photo. Lead with the production board.
**First-day plan:** reply to every comment; ask a few printer friends / your email list to check it out (don't beg for upvotes — PH penalizes that; just share the link).

## Part 2 — Founder-story PR pitch

"A working screen printer built his own shop software" is a genuinely pitchable story for print-trade pubs (Impressions, Printwear/PRINTHOUSE, Screen Printing Mag), small-business/maker press, and local Reno business media. Each pickup is a strong backlink.

**Subject line options:**
- A screen printer got fed up with his software and built his own
- How a Reno print shop built the tool the industry didn't

**Pitch email:**
> Hi [Name] —
>
> Quick pitch that might fit [publication]: I'm a screen printer in Reno, and after years of running my shop on spreadsheets and software that never quite fit, I built my own — InkTracker. It's now used by other shops around the country.
>
> The angle I think your readers would like isn't "new software launched" — it's *why a working printer ended up writing his own tools*, and what that says about the gap between the software industry and the trades. I can talk specifics: the pricing mistakes that quietly kill shop margins, why per-seat software punishes small shops, how live supplier pricing changes quoting, and building a company while still running the press.
>
> Happy to do an interview, write a guest piece, or just be a source. Real shop, real story — I use the thing every day.
>
> — Joe, Founder, InkTracker · inktracker.app · [phone]

**Where to send it:**
- Print-trade: Impressions Magazine, Printwear, Screen Printing Magazine, Printavo/DecoNetwork-adjacent blogs (as a guest contributor, not a competitor pitch).
- Small-biz/maker: Indie Hackers (post your story yourself), Starter Story, Failory, relevant newsletters.
- Local: Reno Gazette-Journal business desk, Northern Nevada Business Weekly, EDAWN.
- HARO / Qwoted / Featured.com: answer queries about small-business software, QuickBooks, the trades, bootstrapping.

**Assets to have ready:** a headshot, 2–3 shop/product photos, a one-paragraph bio, and the InkTracker one-liner. (You already have the shop B-roll stills and screenshots.)

**Rule:** lead with the *story and the value to their readers*, never "please cover my product." Journalists ignore the second and bite on the first.
