## After it's live: reviews (the actual payoff)

The listing is the container; **reviews** are what rank you and convince buyers. Kick this off the day each profile goes live:

- Both platforms give you an **official review-invitation link/tool** inside the vendor dashboard. Use that — it sends buyers to a verified review flow.
- Email your **10–15 happiest shops** with your review link. Short, personal ask: "We just listed InkTracker on G2/Capterra — if it's helped your shop, a quick honest review would mean a lot."
- **Do not** offer discounts/cash for a review, write them yourself, or have staff post — G2 and Capterra actively detect and remove incentivized/fake reviews, and it can get your profile penalized. Real reviews from real users only.
- Aim for the review threshold each platform needs to show a star rating publicly (typically a small handful) — that first cluster is what turns the listing from a stub into a lead source.
