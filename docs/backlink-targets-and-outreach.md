# InkTracker — Backlink Targets & Outreach

Authority (backlinks + mentions) is your #1 ranking gap. This is the target list of pages that already rank for your terms, plus copy-paste outreach to get InkTracker onto them. Work it top-down.

## The key insight
Many "Top 10 screen printing software" listicles are **auto-generated and pull their lists from the directories** (Capterra, G2, SoftwareConnect, SaaSWorthy). So **the directories are upstream** — getting listed there often gets you onto the listicle farms automatically. Do the directories first, then the editorial pages, then check the farms.

## Rules that keep this working
- **Personalize every send** — reference the actual page/post. Mass-blast asks get ignored or marked spam.
- **Never buy links or use link farms** — Google penalizes it; it'll hurt more than help.
- **Lead with "I'm a printer who built this,"** not features. It's your credibility and it's true.
- **Track it** in a simple sheet: Site · Contact · Sent · Response · Link live? Aim for a handful of quality links a month — that compounds.

## Priority order
1. Group 1 directories (Capterra/G2 first) — upstream authority.
2. Group 2 editorial emails (ConvertCalculator, Screen Print Direct first).
3. Product Hunt launch.
