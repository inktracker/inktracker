## How the model adapts from NorCal

NorCal is a *supply* house (ink, screens, chemicals). S&S, SanMar, and AS Colour are *blanks/
garment* distributors. The two-way structure still holds, but the emphasis flips:

- **NorCal:** lead with the referral commission (they're smaller, a per-signup cut moves them).
- **Blanks distributors:** lead with the **sales-channel + integration-partner** angle. InkTracker
  already pulls their catalog so shops spec and price garments mid-job (shops order blanks
  constantly) — but the actual order still happens on the supplier's own site today. That gap *is*
  the opportunity: turn InkTracker into the reorder channel that links back to them. Getting
  InkTracker listed as an official integration/technology partner and co-marketed to their
  decorator base is worth more to them than a small referral cut — so make that the headline and
  keep the referral commission as a secondary sweetener. Do not claim shops already buy through the
  app; they don't yet — that's what we'd build together.

## Prioritized targets

**1. S&S Activewear — start here (warmest door).**
Why first: you already pull their catalog and styles through their API, and they run an
active technology-partner program (they publicly deepened one with commonsku in 2025). The ask is
low-friction: "list InkTracker as an integration partner and co-market to decorators." Big US
decorator base. Door to knock: their Business Solutions / Customer Integrations (API) team and
marketing-partnerships contact.

**2. SanMar — biggest reach, slowest yes.**
Why: largest US imprintable-apparel distributor, so the prize is the biggest — but they're
enterprise and bureaucratic. They run the **PSST** program for contract decorators and integrate
via **PromoStandards / PromoLink**, so there's a defined integration lane. Play the long game:
get InkTracker into their integration ecosystem, then in front of PSST decorators. Door to knock:
SanMar integrations / PromoStandards contact and the PSST program team.

**3. AS Colour — fastest yes, smaller volume.**
Why: premium blanks brand, smaller US footprint, but relationship-friendly and already publishes a
"recommended printers & embroiderers" partner directory plus wholesale trade accounts. Easiest to
get a "recommended software" listing and a co-marketing email. Door to knock: AS Colour US
wholesale / marketing team.

Suggested cadence: send S&S now, AS Colour same week (fast win to build momentum), SanMar in
parallel knowing it'll take longer.
